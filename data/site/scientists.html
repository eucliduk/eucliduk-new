The mission

Launched from Florida in July 2023, the European Space Agency’s (ESA) Euclid mission explores the composition and evolution of the dark Universe. It is mapping the large-scale structure of the Universe across space and time by observing billions of galaxies out to 10 billion light-years, across more than a third of the sky.

By studying how the Universe has expanded and how structure has formed over cosmic history, Euclid will reveal more about gravity, dark energy and dark matter.

Computing: IRIS

Euclid UK has access to eInfrastructure for Research and Innovation for STFC (IRIS), which provides distributed high-performance computing facilities.

A historical allocation of 1,000 CPU years supported development work by UK-based Euclid members in 2022–2024. Contact the EUCG directly to ask about current computing access.
