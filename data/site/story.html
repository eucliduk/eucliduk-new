Euclid’s journey from launch to new views of the Universe.

July 2023 · Launch

Euclid is a flagship space telescope launched by the European Space Agency in July 2023. While its main mirror is only about half the size of the Hubble Space Telescope's at 1.2 m, Euclid can image a much larger part of the sky in one go — observing more of the Universe in a few days than Hubble will ever see.

May 2024 · A stellar nursery

In May 2024, stunning new views of the nearby Universe as seen by the Euclid space telescope were revealed. Here we see a beautiful cloud of glowing interstellar gas and dust giving birth to brand new stars. Known as Messier 78, this stellar nursery had never before been captured in its entirety with such clarity. 300,000 new objects were uncovered in this image alone, including newly forming planets. Usually hidden from view behind the dust, Euclid used its infrared camera to see through the clouds. Scientists will use this data to learn more about how populations of stars form and change over time.
