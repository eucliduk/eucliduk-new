VR Headsets (Recommended 8+)

Overview:

A journey through the Universe and an introduction to the mystery of missing matter. Watch here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=teyfLZriUvs

Staff required:

1-2 people

If busy, one person should look after the person wearing the headset and another should be managing the queue. Whoever’s managing the queue can display what’s being shown on an iPad as an alternative to anyone waiting nearby.

Introductory chat pointers:

  • This is a ~ 3 min long trip through the Universe. Please remain seated during your journey!
  • As you place the headset on, you can adjust the fit by turning the dial on the lower back of the headset. If the image is blurry, you can adjust this by turning the dial on the front bottom of the headset.
  • Feel free to have a look around in all directions!
  • Let us know if you need any help. If you start to feel motion sick, please take the headset off and a member of our team will be happy to show you the rest of the journey on an iPad.

Risk Assessment TLDR

  • Parent/guardian consent should be obtained prior to a child’s participation
  • The staff member will monitor behaviour and pause the session if anyone stands
  • All headsets are disinfected using wipes during the beginning and end of the day
  • Anyone diagnosed with eye conditions should note it could aggravate symptoms, suggest viewing on iPad as an alternative.
  • Anyone with a history of seizures should seek guidance before participating, suggest viewing on iPad as an alternative.

Back-ups: There are 2x spare headsets if the battery is low. USB-C chargers for the headsets are available behind the exhibit area.

UV Torch: Finding dark matter around Abell 2390

Overview: Participants shine a UV torch toward a large image of Abell 2390. The UV torch reveals the dark matter halo around the galaxy cluster, based on this research paper https://www.euclid-ec.org/euclid-starts-seeing-darkness/

Optionally, participants can work in a team to find the missing matter on a web app https://eucliduk.net/abell2390detectivegame.html (likely as a pair with one person shining the UV torch and one tracing where the dark matter is, under guidance from their partner, on the iPad).

Staff required:

1 person needed to explain and look after the UV torch, monitoring its use

Introductory chat pointers:

  • Here’s some real research that the Euclid team did recently. We mapped the dark matter halo around a galaxy cluster. Can you find it?
  • Take this torch and shine it towards the image. Scan along the image to see if you can find any dark matter. It’ll appear as a purple glow if it’s there!
  • Please be careful not to shine this torch into anyone’s eyes. It’s UV and can damage your eyes if shone in them.

Risk Assessment TLDR

The UV torch cannot be shone into anyone’s eyes and must only be directed towards the Abell 2390 image board. If the torch user is behaving irresponsibly, stop the activity.

Back-ups: There isn’t a back up torch, but it has a long battery life. Charge using its USB-C port if needed. There are USB-C charge points behind the exhibit back wall.
