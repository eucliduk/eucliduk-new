How to join the Euclid Consortium as a UK-based researcher.

How to apply

  1. Prepare your documents

    You need a short CV and an application or covering letter. Your letter must reflect your membership category and meet the UK criteria below.

  2. Request membership

    Follow the consortium’s getting-started guidance and submit an initial membership request through the Euclid Tracking Portal. At this stage, submit the request itself; you do not upload your documents yet.

  3. Upload when invited

    After your request, you will automatically receive a link to your Tracking profile page. Use that page to upload your short CV and application letter.

Category 1: existing connections

For applicants with previous Euclid connections in the UK or another Euclid Consortium member nation. This includes reactivating lapsed membership, moving from abroad or changing status, such as after completing a Euclid PhD.

In your covering letter, outline your previous Euclid connections, the Organisation Units (OUs) and Science Working Groups (SWGs) you are active in, and any declared consortium projects you are involved in.

UK Euclid Consortium Board (ECB) representatives assess these requests, which should be straightforward.

Category 2: new members

For applicants who are new to the Euclid Consortium. Include all of the following in your covering letter:

  • Why you want to join: the activities or areas of work you hope to contribute to.
  • Your added value: skills, data, software, innovative ideas, resources or other relevant contributions.
  • Endorsement: confirm that you, your supervisor or your line manager have discussed the proposed membership and activities with the appropriate SWG and/or OU lead, and that the relevant leads endorse your application and are happy for it to proceed.

Endorsement is required

Applications that do not name the relevant SWG lead(s) and confirm their endorsement will be returned with a request for this information.

Supervisor or line manager’s contribution

Where applicable, name your supervisor or line manager and state their total full-time equivalent (FTE) contribution recorded in the Euclid Tracking Portal.

More than 2 FTE recorded

If your supervisor or line manager has more than 2 FTE of contributed time recorded, your application can be accepted on this basis, subject to the usual endorsement from the relevant SWG and/or OU lead. You do not need to meet the additional criteria below.

No supervisor or line manager above 2 FTE

Additionally explain why your proposed work requires consortium membership and cannot use public Euclid data, including releases planned within the next 6 months.

This also applies to early career researchers, including PhD students and postdocs, even when working with an existing consortium member.

Additional contributions, such as significant time and effort, are viewed favourably. State your expected Euclid time commitment and the membership status of your supervisor, line manager or host.

How requests are assessed

Applications are considered case by case, taking account of the importance and relevance of the proposed work, the added value to Euclid and the applicant’s career stage. Some flexibility may be appropriate for early career researchers.

UK ECB representatives assess Category 2 requests in consultation with the Euclid UK Coordination Group (EUCG), seeking further input from consortium colleagues, such as SWG leads, where appropriate.

After approval

Use the Euclid Consortium quick-start guide to become involved in consortium activities. Authorship rights are based on your contributions to the consortium.

Members are expected to have read the Euclid Consortium Code of Conduct (PDF).
