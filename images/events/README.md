# Event recording thumbnail

`cosmic-detectives-recording.webp` is a locally resized WebP of the official
YouTube thumbnail for The Royal Society’s “What are the Universe’s greatest
mysteries?” recording, linked from the Events page.

- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3pQnSEmCHzc&t=4s
- Thumbnail source: https://i.ytimg.com/vi/3pQnSEmCHzc/maxresdefault.jpg
- Publisher: The Royal Society.

The card opens the Royal Society recording page; the external player and captions have not been audited.

## Festival cards added 11 November 2026

- `newscientist-2026.webp`: official Euclid exhibitor artwork linked from https://live.newscientist.com/exhibitors/euclid-dark-universe-detective (exhibitor image ending `6af94bdc-e57d-4321-931a-6c76e967aa77_3.1_nologos.jpg`).
- `orkney-2026.webp`: official event image, https://oisf.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Euclid-key-visual-image.webp.
- `edinburgh-science-festival-2026.webp`: Dynamic Earth event page image, https://dynamicearth.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/dynamic-earth-earth-from-space.jpg. Euclid activity dates are 7–10 April within the 7–13 April event.
- Swansea uses the existing Dark Universe Detective / Royal Society artwork. Its official page blocked automated reading; no unverified day or time was added.

Downloaded festival images were resized to at most 1280 pixels wide and converted to WebP.

## UK map

The event map silhouette is derived from Natural Earth 1:50m country boundaries (public domain): https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson. The UK geometry is projected to Mercator for matching the city coordinates; the SVG is stored in `assets/site/events-uk-map.svg` and inlined in the event content to follow the site themes and forced colours.
