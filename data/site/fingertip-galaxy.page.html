Fingertip Galaxy

A spiral galaxy made from the painted fingertips of more than 250 scientists and engineers working on Euclid. The artwork was created by Lisa Pettibone and Tom Kitching, engraved onto a plaque and attached to the spacecraft.

This digital recreation traces the individual marks and their positions from the supplied photograph. The lettering follows the panel’s curved layout; a larger original would allow the smallest details to be refined further.

Lettering on the panel

We must get our fingerprints onto the keys, our marks into space, for the ache of it, maybe for survival / There is no such thing as unextraordinary light, ordinary matter.

We light out to observe the darkness visible.

Poem excerpt: Unextraordinary Light (For Euclid) by Simon Barraclough.

The panel is titled “Fingertip Galaxy” and carries the credit “Created by the Euclid Consortium”.

Artwork: Lisa Pettibone, Tom Kitching and the participating Euclid scientists and engineers. Read about the original artwork at ESA.
