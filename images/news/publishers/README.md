# Publisher logos

These logos identify the publishers of linked coverage; they do not imply endorsement.
Retrieved 11 November 2026 and stored locally to avoid third-party image requests.

- `bbc-sky-at-night.svg`: official white masthead from the BBC Sky at Night Magazine storefront, linked through `https://www.skyatnightmagazine.com/` as its `--headerLogo` asset. Preserved unchanged and displayed on a dark background.
- `the-guardian.svg`: official inline masthead from `https://www.theguardian.com/uk`. The page's CSS fill variable is resolved to Guardian navy (`#052962`) so the standalone SVG renders correctly.
- `sky-news.png`: official primary logo linked in the footer of `https://skynewsdistribution.sky/en-gb`, served from `https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltbaa2f7a094b05ef0/blt4a0588043b99dc0b/68f8a5b2cc5b6905724e240f/Sky_News_PRIMARY_RGB.png`. Preserved unchanged.

- `iflscience.svg`: IFLScience wordmark from `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IFL_Science.svg`, sourced there from IFLScience and marked PD-textlogo. Preserved unchanged.
- `the-observer.svg`: official inline masthead from `https://observer.co.uk/news/science-technology/article/the-science-of-serendipity`. The page’s `--text_primary` colour is resolved to black for standalone display; path geometry is unchanged.
